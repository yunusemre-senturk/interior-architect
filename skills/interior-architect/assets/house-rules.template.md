<!-- Examples only. They show the kind of rule a user adds after correcting the assistant. Delete them and write your own. -->
- Cleaning tools (mop, bucket, upright vacuum) get their own closed compartment in the entry unit. - Nowhere else to keep them. - Entry unit.
- No drawers in the entry unit, only doors and shelves. - Preference. - Entry unit.
- Partial hanging only, never a full-height hanging bay; storage below, a small shelf above. - Long coats live in the wardrobe. - Entry unit.
- Module widths symmetric and quiet (equal outer bays, centre bay may differ); the middle door is a single leaf. - Looks calmer from the hall. - Entry unit.
- Shelves sit tight against the back panel; no service gap behind them. - Preference. - All cabinets.
- Do not design around wall sockets behind cabinets; cut the back panel and use the socket from inside. - Simpler. - All cabinets.
- Every surface you see with a door open must be finished. - Old tile and primer must not show. - Niches and closets.
- Always bring 2-3 own ideas and an interactive configurator when a variable decides the design. - I want a partner, not only a reviewer. - All.
