# The details professionals do not forget

Run this list before calling any concept "developed". Each line is a mistake that shows up on site, costs money to fix, and was avoidable on paper. Labels: V = cited guideline or manufacturer, R = regulation, G = general practice. Numbers are cm.

## Doors and openings
- [ ] Which way does every door swing, and what does it hit? Check light switches (put them on the **handle side**, not behind the leaf), wardrobe doors, radiators, window handles and other doors (G).
- [ ] A door stop for every hinged door, so the handle never meets a wall or a glass cabinet (G).
- [ ] Door undercut or transfer grille where a room has extract ventilation or a dryer (V, makers).
- [ ] The finished floor height under the door: new tile or parquet can make an existing door rub. Plan the trim or the threshold (G).
- [ ] Head height under any door or flap that opens above ~185 (G).

## Switches, sockets and data
- [ ] Walk the room in the dark: a switch at every entry, two-way switching for rooms with two doors, and for stairs and long halls (G).
- [ ] Bedside: switch for the main light, socket plus USB-C at each side, reachable from the pillow (G).
- [ ] Sockets inside cabinets for chargers, a router, a robot vacuum dock, an electric toothbrush, a hand-held vacuum (G).
- [ ] Sockets at the planned TV height, behind a wall-hung TV, with a cable conduit down to the media unit (G).
- [ ] No socket hidden behind a fixed unit unless the back panel is cut to use it (see house rules).
- [ ] Data cable or mesh node location for the home office, the TV and the router (G).
- [ ] Sockets for future needs: a car charger route, an induction hob (it may need its own circuit), heated towel rail, electric blinds (G).

## Finishes and junctions
- [ ] Where every material meets another: floor transitions, tile ends, worktop to wall, skirting to cabinet plinth. Draw it if it is not obvious (G).
- [ ] Tile set-out: centre the grid on the main view (the vanity, the niche, the window), not on a corner, so cut tiles land in hidden places. Line up grout with niches, shelves and fixtures where possible (G).
- [ ] Shower niche heights align with a grout line (G).
- [ ] Skirting height and profile chosen together with door trims and cabinet plinths, so lines meet (G).
- [ ] Silicone joints at every change of plane in wet zones; never grout an internal corner (G).
- [ ] Paint sheen: matt on ceilings, washable matt or eggshell on walls in halls and children's rooms, satin or semi-gloss on wet rooms and trims (G).

## Cabinets and joinery
- [ ] Filler strips at walls so doors and drawers clear handles, door frames and the wall (≈ 3-5 cm, more at an out-of-plumb wall) (G).
- [ ] Corner units: blind corners need a filler so drawers on both sides can open (G).
- [ ] Toe kick (plinth recess) so feet fit while standing at the counter (≈ 7-10 deep, ≈ 10 high) (G).
- [ ] Cabinet tops: run to the ceiling with a scribe strip, or stop with a planned dust gap. Never a random 12 cm dust shelf (G).
- [ ] Ventilation paths for the fridge, oven and boiler cabinets, exactly as the manuals say (V).
- [ ] Ironing board, mop, bucket, vacuum, step stool, recycling bins: each has a named home (G).

## Light
- [ ] No downlight directly above a person's head at a mirror, desk or bed pillow; light the face from the front (G).
- [ ] Wall washers and accent lights point at things worth lighting, not at blank corners (G).
- [ ] Lights inside wardrobes, pantries and drawers switch with the door (G).
- [ ] A night path: low-level, warm, motion-triggered light from bed to bathroom (≤ ~10 lux at the eye, warm) (V, circadian guidance; see wellbeing.md).

## Window treatments
- [ ] Stack-back space: curtains need wall space beside the window when open, or they cover the glass (G).
- [ ] Track or rod fixed into a solid header or a ceiling pocket, planned before plaster (G).
- [ ] Blackout for bedrooms, cordless controls where children live (V, CPSC).
- [ ] Radiator under the window: curtains that fall to the floor block the heat; use sill-length or a blind (G).

## Services and access
- [ ] Access hatch to every valve, trap, junction box, meter, and the drain clean-out (G).
- [ ] Leak sensor under the sink, dishwasher, washer and boiler (G).
- [ ] Extract fan with run-on timer or humidity sensor in the bathroom (G).
- [ ] Hood ducting route: the shortest route out with few bends (G).

## Living with it
- [ ] Where do the bins go? The wet umbrella? The post? The shopping bags on arrival? The dog's lead and towel? (G)
- [ ] Where do guests put their coats and bags? (G)
- [ ] Where does the robot vacuum go, and can it pass under the furniture (≈ 10 clear)? (G)
- [ ] How is each surface cleaned? Can the mop reach behind and under? (G)
- [ ] What happens when someone is ill, old, or on crutches for six weeks? (G)
- [ ] Is there one place in the house that can be messy without being seen? (G)
