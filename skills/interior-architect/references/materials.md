# Materials and construction

Labels: V = cited guideline or manufacturer, G = general practice. Names in local markets differ; see the region file for local terms.

## Carcass and fronts (G)
- **Carcass:** usually 18 mm melamine-faced chipboard or MDF. Back panel: 8 mm, or 18 mm where shelves are fixed to it. Edges: 2 mm PVC/ABS on visible edges, 0.8-1 mm inside.
- **Front options:**

| Front | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|
| Melamine MDF | Cheap, durable | - |
| Thermofoil (membrane) | - | Peels near heat; avoid next to ovens |
| Lacquer | Looks good | Chips |
| Acrylic / high gloss | - | Shows scratches and fingerprints |
| Veneer | Warm look | Needs care |

- **Wet zones** (under the sink, bathroom, laundry): moisture-resistant (green) MDF or compact laminate, sealed edges, and a raised plinth with adjustable feet.

## Covering old walls inside niches (G)
| Method | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|
| Tile primer and paint | Cheap | Grout lines still show |
| Remove tile and re-plaster | Clean result | Messy work |
| Microcement over tile | Seamless | Needs a specialist; costs more |
| Thin compact laminate, HPL or melamine lining | Warm look, durable | Takes 0.8-2 cm off the width; count it |

## Shelf spans (V, CPA tables at span/240)
- 18-19 mm board under a heavy load (books, pots) sags beyond ~55-60 cm of single span. Fixes: a middle support, a front rail, or fixing the shelf to the back panel.
- A shallow shelf (<= 30 cm) with its back edge fixed can span about twice as far.
- Light loads (shoes, towels): ~80 cm is fine (G).

## Hardware (G)
- Soft-close hinges. Full-extension runners where the user wants drawers.
- Push-to-open needs a gap behind the door and fails on heavy doors or doors loaded with shelves. Use a hidden finger pull instead.
- Keep door leaves <= 60 wide. Doors taller than ~200 need 3-4 hinges, and they stay flat better if the front is thicker or framed.

## Worktops (G)
| Material | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|---|---|---|
| Compact laminate | Thin, tough | - |
| Quartz composite | Hard | Not for very hot pots |
| Granite | Durable | - |
| Laminate | Cheap | Swells at joints |
| Solid surface | Sinks can be built in seamlessly | - |

Maintenance is part of the choice. For every finish you propose, say how it ages and how it is cleaned.
