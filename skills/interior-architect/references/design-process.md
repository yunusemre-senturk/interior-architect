# The professional design process

How interior architects structure a project, and what the agent should produce at each stage. Use the stage names the user's region knows; the content is the same.

## Stage map (V)
| Here | RIBA Plan of Work 2020 (UK) | AIA phases (US) | TMMOB İçmimarlar Odası (TR, share of fee) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 Brief | 0 Strategic Definition, 1 Preparation and Briefing | Pre-design / programming | - |
| 1 Survey | 1 (site information) | Existing conditions | Rölöve (10 %) |
| 2 Concept | 2 Concept Design | Schematic Design (SD) | Ön proje (20 %) |
| 3 Develop | 3 Spatial Coordination | Design Development (DD) | Kesin proje ve malzeme seçimi (30 %) |
| 4 Detail | 4 Technical Design | Construction Documents (CD) | Uygulama projesi, sistem ve imalat detayları (35 %) |
| 5 Tender | (procurement) | Bidding / negotiation | Mahal listesi, metraj, iş programı (5 %) |
| 6 Build | 5 Manufacturing and Construction | Construction Administration (CA) | (site supervision, separate) |
| 7 Handover | 6 Handover, 7 Use | Close-out | - |

The Turkish percentages show where the effort sits: detailing and development carry about two-thirds of the work. A concept without them is not a buildable design.

## What the agent delivers at each stage
**0 Brief** - household profile, day-in-the-life notes, requirement list, budget band, timeline, decision-maker, what stays. Output: a one-page brief the user confirms (see discovery-brief.md, assets/brief-questionnaire.md).

**1 Survey** - measuring list and survey sheet; every number M or P; photos; services located (survey-checklist.md).

**2 Concept** - 2-3 genuinely different concepts (innovation-playbook.md), each as: idea in one sentence, a rough plan, what it gains and loses, who it suits, cost band (low / mid / high). One recommendation. Output format: assets/concept-board.template.md.

**3 Develop** - the chosen concept with real dimensions, furniture and circulation checked open and closed, services placed, lighting plan, material and colour palette with samples to order, budget estimate (assets/budget-sheet.template.md).

**4 Detail** - only when asked or when fit depends on it: elevations, sections, joinery drawings, socket and switch plan with heights, tile set-out, door schedule, finishes schedule (mahal listesi), 1:5-1:10 details for joints that decide fit.

**5 Tender** - a scope and finish list precise enough that 2-3 makers can quote the same thing; questions to ask each one; how to compare quotes (project-delivery.md).

**6 Build** - sequence of trades, lead times, what to check at each site visit, change control.

**7 Handover** - snag list (assets/snag-list.template.md), manuals and warranties, maintenance notes for each finish, a "how this home works" note for the household.

## Rules of the process
- Do not jump to finishes before the function works (zones → circulation → storage → services → light → materials).
- Each stage ends with a decision the user makes. Log it in the design log.
- After the user picks a concept, stop offering alternatives; develop the chosen one.
- A revision that changes a decided item must say so and say why.
