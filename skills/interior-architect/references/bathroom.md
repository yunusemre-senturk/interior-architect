# Bathroom

Labels: V = cited guideline or manufacturer, R = regulation, G = general practice. Numbers are in cm. Numbers from NKBA have the inch value in brackets.

## Clearances (V, NKBA: recommended / never below)
- WC centreline to a wall or fixture: 46 [18"] / 38 [15"].
- Clear space in front of the WC, basin or shower: 76 [30"] / 53 [21"].
- Shower inside: 90 × 90 [36" × 36"] / 76 × 76 [30" × 30"]. Keep the shower head zone at least 203 cm high.
- Basin centreline to a side wall: 51 [20"] comfortable / 38 [15"].
- WC seat height: 38-48 [15"-19"].
- Vanity height: 81-91, or set it for the main user (G).

## Storage
- Keep wall cabinets above the WC shallow so nobody bumps a head or shoulder.
- Daily items between 90 and 150 cm high. Wet and heavy items at the bottom.
- Use moisture-resistant boards with sealed edges and a raised plinth (see materials.md).

## Light and mirror
- Light the mirror from both sides or from the front at face height, not only from above, and never from behind the head.
- Aim for 300-500 lux on the face and CRI >= 90 (see lighting.md).

## Safety
- Electrical zones apply. No ordinary sockets in zones 0-2. Every bathroom circuit goes on a 30 mA RCD. The details are in systems-safety.md and the region file.
- A windowless bathroom or WC needs a ventilation shaft or mechanical extract. This is a regulation in most regions.
- Give the floor slip resistance. Fit grab rails, or blocking in the wall for future rails, near the WC and shower if older people live or visit there (G).
