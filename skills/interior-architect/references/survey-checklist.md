# Site survey checklist

A number read from a plan is not a measured one. Mark every number **M** (measured) or **P** (read from plan). The user can fill in `assets/survey-sheet.md` on a phone.

- [ ] **Walls:** measure each wall at two heights (~100 cm and near the ceiling). Measure both diagonals to check the room is square, and note any wall that is out of plumb.
- [ ] **Ceiling:** net height at 3 or more points. Note beams, soffits, cornices and pipes that lower it. Record the height of the gas pipe.
- [ ] **Doors and windows:** for each, record width, head height, sill height, which way it opens, and distance from the corners. Also record radiator and window-handle positions, and which window sashes open.
- [ ] **Services:** record each one with its height above the finished floor: water supply, drain, gas valve, boiler and flue, sockets, switches, junction boxes, distribution board, ventilation grilles and shafts.
- [ ] **Surfaces:** for every wall that will stay visible, record its condition: tile, primer, putty or damage.
- [ ] **Floor:** planned finish thickness. Tile or parquet changes every height above it.
- [ ] **Photos:** every wall, straight on, plus close-ups of every service point.
- [ ] **What is behind the walls:** neighbour rooms, external wall, shared wall with a bedroom (noise), load-bearing or not.
- [ ] **Appliances:** model and size of every appliance that goes into the space.
