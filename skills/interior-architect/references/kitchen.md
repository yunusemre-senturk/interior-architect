# Kitchen

Labels: V = cited guideline or manufacturer, R = regulation, G = general practice. Numbers are cm. Each NKBA number has its inch value in brackets.

## Circulation (V, NKBA)
- Work aisle: >= 107 [42"] for one cook, >= 122 [48"] for two. Walkway: >= 91 [36"].
- In small rooms, aim for 107. If you go below it, say so and say what it costs.
- Check the aisle with the dishwasher, oven and fridge doors open, not only closed.

## Layout
- Do not put a full-height unit (fridge, pantry, oven tower) between two main work centres (V).
- Single-wall kitchens do not use the work triangle. Put the sink in the middle (V).
- Never put a hob at the end of a counter against a tall unit or a wall unless there is landing space on both sides. Keep the hob away from windows and curtains, and check the local gas rule (V/R).

## Landing areas (V, NKBA)
- Sink: 61 [24"] on one side and 46 [18"] on the other. Prep zone of about 91 × 61 next to the sink.
- Hob: 30 [12"] on one side and 38 [15"] on the other, at the same height as the hob.
- Fridge: 38 [15"] on the handle side.
- Oven or microwave: 38 [15"] next to it or above it.
- Dishwasher: within 91 [36"] of the sink edge, with about 53 [21"] of standing room beside the open door.

## Heights (V/G)
- Work surface ≈ elbow height minus 10-15. Hob ≈ elbow height minus 20. Typical counters are 85-95 high and 60 deep. Regional norms are in the region file.
- Raising the dishwasher 15-30 cm cuts bending, but costs landing space and wall space (V).
- Oven at chest or eye height, or on telescopic rails. Microwave base >= 38 above the floor, ideally just below the shoulder (V).
- Wall units: underside 45-60 above the counter (G).
- Hood above the hob: gas >= 65, electric or induction >= 50-55 (V). The manufacturer's manual wins. Make the hood at least as wide as the hob (G).
- Counter seating: about 61 [24"] of width per person, and about 38 [15"] of knee depth at a 91 cm counter (V).

## Services
- One socket per appliance, plus 2 free double sockets on the worktop. Worktop sockets go above the backsplash, never behind the hob or sink (G).
- Duct the hood outside where possible. Recirculating hoods need their filters changed.
- Fridge, oven and built-in appliances need the ventilation gaps given by the manufacturer.

## Precedent questions to ask
How many people cook at once? Does anyone cook daily or rarely? Is there a big weekly shop (pantry volume)? Which appliances stand on the counter every day? Left- or right-handed main cook?
