# Accessibility, ageing in place, and children

Labels: V = cited guideline, R = regulation, G = general practice. Numbers in cm with inches in brackets. Region codes may be stricter; see the region file.

## Universal design (V, NC State Center for Universal Design, 7 principles)
Equitable use · flexibility in use · simple and intuitive · perceptible information · tolerance for error · low physical effort · size and space for approach and use.
In practice: lever handles and D-pulls instead of knobs; no thresholds; contrast at edges and steps; a seat where shoes go on; everyday items reachable without a stool; blocking in bathroom walls for future grab rails.

- Grab rails need blocking (solid backing) behind the tiles. Fit it during any bathroom renovation, even if rails are not wanted yet (G).
- Curbless showers need a recessed floor or a raised bathroom floor; plan it before demolition (G).
- Contrast: ~30 LRV points between floor and wall, WC and wall, grab rail and wall (V, UK practice).
- Lighting: older eyes need roughly two to three times more light for the same task; add task light and avoid glare from shiny floors (G).
- Prefer a step-free route from the entrance to one bedroom, one bathroom and the kitchen (G).

## Children (V, US CPSC guidance unless marked)
- **Tip-over:** anchor dressers, bookcases, TV stands, wardrobes and TVs to the wall. It is critical in seismic regions (see region file).
- **Windows:** limit openings to ≤ 10 cm [4"] with stops or guards where children can reach, while keeping at least one window per room usable for fire escape.
- **Blinds:** cordless window coverings only; strangulation risk from loops and bead chains.
- **Stairs:** hardware-mounted (screwed) gates at the top of stairs; pressure gates only at the bottom or between rooms. Gates suit about 6 months to 2 years.
- **Baths:** never leave a child unattended; thermostatic mixer to cap hot water at ~38-41 °C (G).
- **Furniture edges:** rounded corners on low tables; corner bumpers that stay on.
- **Balustrades:** no gaps a 10 cm sphere can pass, no horizontal rails that act as a ladder (R in most codes).
- **Sockets:** shuttered sockets (standard in many countries) or covers (G).
- **Design to grow:** a child's room changes every ~3-5 years. Plan a bed wall that fits cot → single → double, shelves that move, a desk that comes later, and storage low enough for the child to tidy up by themselves (G).

## Pets (G)
- Feeding station out of the walkway, with a wipeable floor and splashback.
- Dog-wash or hand shower near the entrance.
- Scratch-resistant upholstery and floors (porcelain tile, hard-wax oiled oak, avoid soft pine).
- Litter tray in a ventilated cabinet with a cat flap and extract.
