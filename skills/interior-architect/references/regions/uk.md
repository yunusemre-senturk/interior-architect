# United Kingdom (UK)

**Status: skeleton.** The points below come from memory and have not been checked against current editions. Tell the user this before relying on them. Building regulations differ across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. Contributions welcome.

## Electrical
- BS 7671, Section 701 (bathrooms): uses the same zones as IEC. Socket outlets are allowed in a bathroom only more than 3 m from the boundary of zone 1. This is stricter than many countries.
- Part P (England): new circuits, consumer unit changes and work in bathroom zones are notifiable. Use a registered competent person.

## Gas
- Any gas work must be done by a **Gas Safe registered** engineer. This is a legal requirement.

## Ventilation (Approved Document F, England; verify edition)
Typical intermittent extract rates:

| Room | Extract rate |
|---|---|
| Kitchen | 30 l/s with a cooker hood, 60 l/s otherwise |
| Bathroom | 15 l/s |
| Utility room | 30 l/s |

## Accessibility
- Approved Document M covers access and use. The contrast guidance (about 30 LRV points) is described in aesthetics.md.
