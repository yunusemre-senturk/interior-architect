# Türkiye (TR)

**Status:** full. Sources checked 2026-11. Regulations change; confirm the current edition and the local gas distributor's rules. Gas and electrical work is decided by a licensed installer (yetkili tesisatçı / elektrikçi).

## Regulations (R)
**Planlı Alanlar İmar Yönetmeliği.** This regulation binds new buildings. For renovations, use it as a benchmark.
- Art. 28, inner height of habitable floors: >= 2.60 m. Bathrooms, WCs, pantries and corridors may go down to 2.20 m.
- Art. 29, minimum rooms (area / shortest side):

| Room | Area | Shortest side |
|---|---|---|
| Living | 12 m² | 3.00 m |
| Bedroom | 9 m² | 2.50 m |
| Kitchen | 3.3 m² | 1.50 m |
| Bathroom | 3 m² | 1.50 m |
| WC | 1.2 m² | 1.00 m |

  Halls and corridors: >= 1.20 m wide.
- Art. 32: a windowless bathroom or WC must have a ventilation shaft or mechanical ventilation.
- Art. 31, stairs: stairs inside a dwelling >= 1.00 m wide; common stairs >= 1.20 m. Riser <= 0.16 m in buildings without a lift, <= 0.18 m with a lift. Going from 2a + b = 60-64 cm, and never < 0.27 m. Winders: >= 0.10 m at the narrow end and >= 0.27 m at the middle. No projecting nosings. Handrails on both sides to TSE accessibility standards. Spaces under stairs lower than 1.80 m must be closed off.
- Art. 34(10): every kitchen gets at least one hood (aspiratör) flue.
- Art. 39, doors: height >= 2.10 m. Width: flat entrance >= 1.00 m, rooms >= 0.90 m, WC and balcony 0.80 m.

## Natural gas (V/R)
Istanbul follows **İGDAŞ Doğal Gaz İç Tesisat Teknik Esasları** (2017 edition). Distributors in other cities (Başkentgaz, İzmirgaz and others) publish their own versions, which are similar in substance. Check the local one.
- A room-sealed combi in a cabinet needs >= 10 cm clear at the sides and front, >= 30 cm above, and a removable front.
- B-type (open-flued) appliances are not allowed in bedrooms, bathrooms or WCs. The room needs >= 8 m³ of volume and a permanent vent of 150 cm².
- Flexible hob hose: <= 150 cm. Every appliance needs a valve that is easy to reach.
- No gas pipes under plaster. Pipes inside a closed channel need ventilated covers.
- Keep gas pipes >= 15 cm from electrical lines.
- Moving a gas point means a new gas project, approved by the distributor and carried out by a licensed firm.

## Electrical (V/G)
- Bathroom zones follow TS HD 60364-7-701 and the Elektrik İç Tesisleri Yönetmeliği: no ordinary sockets in zones 0-2, IPX4 or better in zones 1-2, 30 mA RCD (kaçak akım rölesi) on bathroom circuits.
- Typical heights (G; the MEGEP training module gives sockets at 40-50):

| Item | Height |
|---|---|
| Socket | ~40 cm |
| Switch | ~100-110 cm |
| Worktop socket | ~110-120 cm |

  Where the architect's electrical sheet gives an exact height, follow the sheet.

## Earthquake (V/G)
Turkey is seismic. Anchor tall units, wardrobes, bookcases and TV units to the wall with L-brackets or anti-tip straps into masonry or concrete (not into plasterboard alone), and put heavy items low. Put child-proof latches on the doors of upper cabinets that hold glass. Do not hang heavy frames or mirrors above beds and sofas. Keep the escape route from bedrooms to the front door free of tall furniture. AFAD recommends fixing furniture; a Turkish survey (DergiPark) found that most households take no such measures, so raise it every time.

## Profession and fees (V)
- Interior architecture is a regulated profession under **TMMOB İçmimarlar Odası**. Its minimum-fee tariff splits the design service into: rölöve 10 %, ön proje 20 %, kesin proje ve malzeme seçimi 30 %, uygulama projesi + sistem ve imalat detayları 35 %, mahal listesi + metraj + iş programı 5 %. Minimum fee = approx. area × unit cost (TL/m²) × PİD ratio × HBO share × coordination factor. Use it to explain to users what a full service contains (see design-process.md).
- Market prices change quickly with inflation. Give price ranges only with a source and a date, and suggest 2-3 itemised quotes.

## Market and practice (G)
- Kitchen counters are typically ~90 high and 60 deep.
- Ready-made shoe cabinets (ayakkabılık) are 28-35 deep.
- Carcass is suntalam (melamine chipboard) or MDF-lam. Fronts: MDF-lam, membran (thermofoil), lake (lacquer), akrilik, ahşap kaplama (veneer).
- Washer/dryer stacking: some brand groups (for example the Arçelik group) allow stacking only within their own brands and only when the depths match. Check the manual.
- Guests often take shoes off at the door. Plan space for slippers (misafir terliği) in the entry.
- Radiators (petek) sit under most windows. Check how far they project before running a built-in up to them.
