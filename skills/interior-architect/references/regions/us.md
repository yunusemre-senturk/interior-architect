# United States (US)

**Status: skeleton. These points come from memory and have not been checked against current code editions.** Tell the user this before relying on them. Local amendments to the codes are common, and the local building department and licensed trades decide. Contributions welcome (see CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Units
US users work in inches. Give cm and inches together. The NKBA numbers in the room files already show inches in brackets.

## Building (IRC, verify edition)
- Habitable rooms: minimum ceiling height of about 7 ft (213 cm) (IRC R305).
- WC: about 15" (38 cm) from the centreline to a side wall and 21" (53 cm) clear in front. These match the NKBA minimums.

## Electrical (NEC, verify edition)
- GFCI protection: kitchens, bathrooms, laundry areas, and receptacles near sinks (NEC 210.8).
- Kitchen countertops: receptacles spaced so that no point along the wall line is more than 24" (61 cm) from one. Any counter space 12" wide or more needs a receptacle (NEC 210.52(C)).
- Dedicated circuits: kitchen small-appliance circuits (20 A), a bathroom circuit and a laundry circuit (NEC 210.11(C)).

## Gas
- Gas work follows IFGC or NFPA 54 as adopted locally, and needs a licensed plumber or gas fitter plus a permit.

## Practice (G)
- Standard counter height is 36" (91 cm), depth 25" (63.5 cm).
