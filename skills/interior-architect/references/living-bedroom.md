# Living, dining, bedroom, circulation

Labels: V = cited guideline or manufacturer, R = regulation, G = general practice. Numbers are cm.

## Dining (G)
- Behind dining chairs: 90-100 to a wall or furniture. 75 is tight and only works where nobody passes.
- Table width: 60 per person.

## Living (G)
- Sofa to coffee table: ~40-45.
- Main walking routes: >= 90.
- TV viewing distance depends on screen size and resolution. Put the centre of the screen near seated eye height (~100-110).
- Seats facing the entrance with their backs covered feel safer (prospect-refuge).

## Bedroom (G)
- Leave 60-70 free beside the bed on each side that is used. Less than ~50 at the foot of the bed is tight, especially if it is the only route to the window.
- Leave 90 in front of wardrobe doors. Drawers need their own depth plus ~45.
- Keep reading lights and switches within reach of the bed. Do not put a bare light source in view from the pillow.
- Shared wall with a kitchen, combi boiler or bathroom: check the noise at night.

## Circulation
- Halls and passages: >= 90 (G). Many new-building codes require halls >= 120 and room doors >= 90 clear (R; see the region file).
- Check the width that is left while a door or drawer is open, not only the closed state. 75-80 for a moment, while one door is open, is tight but workable.
