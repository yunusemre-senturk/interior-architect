# Laundry (washer and dryer niches)

Labels: V = cited guideline or manufacturer, R = regulation, G = general practice. All numbers are in cm.

## Layout
- Two machines side by side need about 120 cm of wall. If the niche is narrower, stack them: washer below, dryer above (V, makers).
- To stack, use the maker's stacking kit, or a separate shelf that carries the load. Many makers allow stacking only within their own brand group and only when the two depths match: a 60 cm dryer does not go on a 40-45 cm slim washer. Check the manual for both models (V).
- Place the upper machine so the centre of its door sits at or below the shorter user's shoulder (≈ 0.82 × stature).
- **Confirm both appliances, with model and depth, before drawing anything.**

## Clearances and vibration (V/G)
- Do not put anything on top of the dryer.
- Leave 1-2 cm at the sides so the washer's spin does not shake the cabinet.
- The plug, water valve and drain must be reachable without pulling the machines out. A recessed washer box (valve + drain) is tidier than an exposed trap.
- Give each machine its own earthed socket, ideally on its own circuit.

## Dryer in a closet (V)
- Room temperature 5-35 °C.
- Do not put a dryer behind a door that locks.
- Ventilation: undercut the door and add top and bottom openings or a fan. If none of these is possible, leave the door ajar while the dryer runs. A heat-pump dryer suits closets best.

## Shelf between the machines
- A shelf that carries a dryer (~50 kg plus running vibration) is structural. It needs steel angles or an 18 mm divider panel anchored to the masonry. Check that the dryer's manual allows shelf mounting.
- Gain: no need to match brand and depth, the washer's vibration is isolated, and the washer can be pulled out on its own. Cost: the structure, and possibly the stacking warranty.

## Optional ideas
- Pull-out tray at about 85-90 cm as a landing and folding surface.
- A narrow side column for the ironing board, detergents and a hamper.
- Leak sensor with a shut-off valve, an energy-monitoring plug for a "cycle finished" alert, and LED lighting triggered by the door.
