---
description: Generate 2-3 genuinely different, inventive design concepts with one recommendation
argument-hint: "[room + key facts, e.g. kitchen 250x310, window on long wall]"
---
Use the interior-architect skill. Develop concepts for: $ARGUMENTS

1. Check the brief, house rules, design log and region file. If measurements or appliances are missing, list exactly what is needed and state assumptions clearly.
2. Generate 2-3 concepts, each on a different organizing idea from `references/innovation-playbook.md` (not cosmetic variants).
3. Check each against hard constraints, the six lenses and `references/detail-checklist.md` before presenting.
4. Present with `assets/concept-board.template.md`: idea, plan sketch (SVG or text), gains, losses, who it suits, cost band, comparison table.
5. Make ONE recommendation and say why. Add 2-3 of your own optional ideas (gain + cost).
6. If one variable decides the design, offer a configurator from `assets/configurator.html`.
7. For important decisions, ask the `design-critic` agent to review the recommended concept and report its findings.
