Use the interior-architect skill. Create a material and colour palette for: $ARGUMENTS

1. Read the style already chosen (house rules, design log). Do not reinvent it.
2. Decide the undertone family (warm / cool) and apply 60-30-10 (`references/furnishing-styling.md`).
3. Propose 2-3 main materials plus one accent, with LRV ranges, sheen per surface, and contrast for safety (~30 LRV points at floor/wall and steps).
4. For each finish: where it goes, why, how it ages, how it is cleaned, health and sustainability notes (`references/materials.md`, `references/sustainability.md`).
5. Show it as an inline swatch board (HTML/SVG) if the host renders it; otherwise as a table.
6. List the samples to order and how to test them (large samples, morning and evening light, next to the real floor).
