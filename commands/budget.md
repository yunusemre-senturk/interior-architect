description: Estimate and structure a renovation budget, sequence of works and lead times
argument-hint: "[project scope, region, budget band]"
---
Use the interior-architect skill. Build a budget and plan of works for: $ARGUMENTS

1. Use `references/project-delivery.md` and the region file.
2. Fill `assets/budget-sheet.template.md` with line items. Never state a price as fact without a local source and a date; if web search is available, find current local ranges and cite them; otherwise leave prices blank and explain how to get quotes.
3. Add a contingency (10-15 %, or 15-20 % for homes older than ~30 years or when walls are opened).
4. Give the order of works, long-lead items to order early, permits and licensed trades needed.
5. Show where to save without losing quality, and what must not be cut (waterproofing, electrics, ventilation, anchoring).
